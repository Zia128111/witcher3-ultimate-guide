# The Witcher 3 Ultimate Guide

An unofficial, single-file companion site for **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt**, **Hearts of Stone** and **Blood and Wine**.

Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser. There is no build step and no dependencies. Progress, favorites, saved builds and settings are stored in your browser (`localStorage`) and can be exported or imported as JSON from **My Journal**.

## What's inside

- **Interactive map** of White Orchard, Velen and Novigrad, Skellige, Kaer Morhen and Toussaint, with 1,219 markers and found-tracking
- **World Explorer**, with journal locations linked to map pins
- **Quest journal**: the main story as a path, contracts on a notice board, walkthrough steps you can tick off, and missable warnings
- **Remastered skill trees** (80 nodes), with a planner, mutagen slots, mutations and a hit forecast
- **Armory**: witcher school sets, upgrade paths, a weapon rack and a craftsmen ladder
- **Bestiary codex** with weakness wheels and hunt kits
- **Alchemist's workbench** with brewing rings and a satchel shopping list
- **Gwent cabinet**: all 186 cards, collection tracking and a deck table
- Collectibles, achievements, money making, DLC and New Game+ guides

## Data and accuracy

Game data was compiled from the Witcher fandom wiki and the Fextralife wiki. Text is paraphrased, and each record links to its source. Records are tagged as wiki-sourced, community guide or unverified, and known gaps are listed in the in-app **Sources and Data** page and in `HANDOFF.md`. Treat community data as "verify in game".
